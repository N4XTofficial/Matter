Create an account or sign in on this device. Passwords are salted and hashed in acc.txt; the actual password is never written there.

Local account only; this is not a cloud account and won't sync between devices.

🔐 What this account does

Shows your username and avatar in Matter's top bar and account panel. It does not create a server login, protect data with a password, or connect you to real chat users.

For a real multi-device account system, Matter would need a hosted backend and secure authentication service.
